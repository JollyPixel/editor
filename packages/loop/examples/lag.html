Sustained overload is the one to watch. Burning more than the step budget per frame makes every frame panic: dropped climbs, and steps per frame pins to the dashed budget line instead of climbing with the backlog. The frame rate settles low — the work really is slow — but it settles. Raise maxStepsPerFrame without the budget and each late frame would schedule more steps than the last, which is the spiral this policy exists to prevent.
