## 1.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [#1088](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/pull/1088) [`ce24126`](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/commit/ce241266470accee7375a2f45745c0c71f0ce1e1) Thanks [@fraxken](https://github.com/fraxken)! - `ui` elements now register from `@jolly-pixel/ui/elements/<name>` (class and dialog helpers included) and the facade, with its types, moved to `@jolly-pixel/ui/facade`; `./feedback` and `./icon` are removed, and the root entry registers nothing and exports element classes as types only.

## 1.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#1061](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/pull/1061) [`d225112`](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/commit/d2251127dac7d6b2ca8f4bf123331062156d645f) Thanks [@fraxken](https://github.com/fraxken)! - `OrbitFlyCamera` takes its fly and look keys from `OrbitFlyKeys` (`keys` option and setter).
  A tour body span such as `` `{camera.forward}` `` shows the key bound to that action, served by `lantern.keys`.
  `KeyChord.format()` names a bare modifier key, and controls exports `isKeyCode` and `MODIFIER_KEY_CODES`.

- [#1004](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/pull/1004) [`e9ac1e6`](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/commit/e9ac1e683040f050fc92d1d5a4276548fc9140c4) Thanks [@fraxken](https://github.com/fraxken)! - Add `followUp` to `Tour`: the last step offers a "Next tour" button that starts the follow-up.
  Add `lantern.offer(tour)` and the `autoStart` option to start a tour the user has not seen yet.
  Fix the Enter key that starts a tour also skipping its first step.

- [#1085](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/pull/1085) [`9877244`](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/commit/9877244fe55ec3890b9fafccb2606dfc72ceaa6c) Thanks [@fraxken](https://github.com/fraxken)! - A tour step takes a `when` condition: Next and Back skip it while it is false, and the counter leaves it out.
  `TourRun` gains `position` and `count`, and `first` and `last` ignore hidden steps.

- [#1058](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/pull/1058) [`4c5b9b7`](https://github.com/JollyPixel/editor/commit/4c5b9b700f4b4b7d4151d13ddbb5dd22a859abce) Thanks [@fraxken](https://github.com/fraxken)! - Rename `LanternSession` to `TourRun` (`lantern.current`, `element.run`); `TourRun` and `LanternSeen` are exported as types only.
  A run cut short by `Lantern` now ends with the `"interrupted"` outcome, and `run.seek()` replaces `run.page` and `run.warn()`.
