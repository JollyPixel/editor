`TreeNode.selectable: false` turns a `jolly-tree` row into a heading: a click or Enter folds it, and it is never selected, activated, renamed, dragged or given a context menu.
`jolly-property-row`, `jolly-separator` and field descriptions follow `--jolly-field-inset-start`, so they stay aligned with field labels at any inset.
