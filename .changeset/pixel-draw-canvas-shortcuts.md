Keyboard handling moves to the host: `PixelArtCanvas.shortcuts` exposes the mode-routed intents and the pan and line modifiers.
The `keybindings` option and getter, `Keybindings`, `DEFAULT_KEYBINDINGS`, `KEYBINDING_ACTIONS` and the keybinding errors are removed.
