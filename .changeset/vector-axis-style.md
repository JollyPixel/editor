Vector, quaternion and transform fields gain `axisStyle` (`"corner"`, `"chip"` or `"letter"`, also a `Defaults` entry and a binding option) to show the axis letter at the input's leading edge.
Peer chips move from over the value's corner to the end of the label cell, and stacked rows put chips and revert at the label line's right.
