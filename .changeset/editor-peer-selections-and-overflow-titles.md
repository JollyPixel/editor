Open asset rooms broadcast a fresh snapshot when their content is replaced from outside (`AssetStateStore` emits `replaced`), and `peerPresence.uvSelections` outlines each peer's selected UV region below the local selection.
Truncated `@jolly-pixel/ui` labels show their full text on hover (`overflow-title="off"` disables it), `jolly-pane-group` tabs collapse to their icon when the strip is too narrow, and `jolly-tree` takes `swatch-position="start"`.
