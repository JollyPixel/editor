Add `jolly-pane-group`: dock layouts store panes as tab groups, a dragged pane joins a group when dropped on a header or tab strip, and `jolly-pane-visibility` reports shown panes.
An empty dock is now a placeholder from its first render and previews its size while a drag arms it.
`jolly-pane` gains an `icon`, shown in its header and its group tab.
